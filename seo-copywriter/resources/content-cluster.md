## Internal Linking Strategy

### Rules
1. Supporting articles link **up** to the pillar with descriptive anchors.
2. Pillar links **down** to every supporting and commercial page in relevant sections.
3. Supporting articles link **across** to related siblings where helpful (not forced).
4. Commercial pages link **back** to relevant guides for trust, and to sibling offers.
5. Prefer natural contextual links over raw "related posts" dumps.
