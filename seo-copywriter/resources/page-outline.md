- [ ] Nearby context without invented landmarks

## Final Pass
- [ ] No fabricated facts, stats, reviews, or prices
- [ ] Reads as human, not SEO textbook
- [ ] Satisfies the search intent
- [ ] Gives a visitor a reason to stay and a reason to act
